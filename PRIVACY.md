# Privacy Policy

**Time Strip** — effective 1 October 2026

Time Strip does not collect, store or share any personal data. This policy covers the app and its
widget on Mac, iPhone and iPad.

- **No network access.** Time Strip makes no network requests. On the Mac, the app and its widget
  run in the macOS App Sandbox without network permission, so they cannot send anything off your
  Mac.
- **Your cities stay on your device.** The system saves the cities you choose as part of the
  widget's configuration. Time Strip does not send them anywhere.
- **No location.** Time Strip never asks for your location. It shows the time zones you choose,
  using your device's clock.
- **No analytics, tracking, advertising or accounts.** The app contains no third-party code.

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new effective date.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/lysyi3m/time-strip/issues).
