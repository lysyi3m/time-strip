# Privacy Policy

**Time Strip** — effective 23 September 2026

Time Strip does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **No network access.** The app and its widget run in the macOS App Sandbox without network
  permission, so they cannot send anything off your Mac.
- **Your cities stay on your Mac.** macOS saves the cities you choose as part of the widget's
  configuration. Time Strip does not send them anywhere.
- **No location.** Time Strip never asks for your location. It shows the time zones you choose,
  using your Mac's clock.
- **No analytics, tracking, advertising or accounts.** The app contains no third-party code.

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new effective date.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/lysyi3m/time-strip/issues).
